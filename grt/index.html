Geometric Reciprocity: Unlocking Self-Supervision
for Stereoscopic Video Generation

ICML 2026

Jingyi Lu, Kai Han
Visual AI Lab, The University of Hong Kong

Paper OpenReview Code Data BibTeX

Geometric Reciprocity turns geometric consistency in stereo video into self-supervision. It predicts disocclusion regions through reciprocal warping, then learns to complete the missing content for stereoscopic video generation.

Reciprocal Geometry for Stereo Video

The method estimates where pixels become invisible under view synthesis and uses those regions as supervision for video inpainting.

Geometric reciprocity exposes disocclusion masks from paired warping directions.

Left-to-right warping reveals the missing regions that need stereo-aware completion.

Self-Supervised Completion

GRT provides a scalable training signal for stereoscopic video generation without requiring ground-truth stereo videos.
